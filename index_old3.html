Questions

What do you need from me?

Just the itinerary: a link, a PDF or a list of stops. For walking and cycling tours, a GPX file gives the most exact line; for waymarked trails I can usually find the official route myself. Send your logo and brand colours if you have them.

How long does it take?

A first draft within 5 working days of receiving the itinerary. Larger packs are scheduled together, and rush delivery in 48 hours is available.

Can it match our brand?

Yes. Route colours, map style, font, panel colours, your logo and your own vehicle icons can all be set to match, and they’re saved as your house style for every future video.

Who owns the videos?

You can use them in your own marketing, on any channel, for as long as you like. The map data credit (© OpenStreetMap contributors) stays in the corner, as the map licence requires.

Is there music or a voice-over?

Videos are delivered silent, so they autoplay anywhere, including on your website. Add your own track, or ask about adding a licensed one.

What if the itinerary changes next season?

Updates are quick because your route is saved. Catalogue clients get updates included.
