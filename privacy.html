Privacy notice

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This notice explains what happens to the details you send through this website.

What is collected

  • What you type into the sample request, contact or feedback forms: your name, company, email address, the tour link, your answers and your notes.
  • If we made a sample for your company without being asked, the business contact details published on your website.
  • Itineraries, logos and route files you send by email.
  • If you accept analytics cookies: how you use this website, such as the pages you visit, how you arrived, your device type and your approximate location, collected by Google Analytics.

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages people find useful, and only if you accept it in the cookie notice. Until you do, no analytics cookies are set and Google Analytics isn’t loaded.

If you accept, Google Analytics sets two cookies, _ga and _ga_<ID>, which tell repeat visits apart and last up to 2 years. We don’t use its advertising features. Your choice is remembered in your browser for 12 months, and you can change it at any time with the “Cookie settings” link at the bottom of every page.

We use no advertising cookies. The fonts are hosted on this site, so your browser doesn’t contact other companies to load them.

Why, and on what basis

We use your details only to reply to your request, make your sample or videos, and send quotes and invoices. For Google Analytics the legal basis is your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time. For everything else, the legal basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, and then performing that contract (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).

If we contact you about a sample we made for your company, or you send us feedback on one, we use your business contact details and your answers to follow up and to improve the service. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in offering our service to tour operators and improving it (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). We quote feedback publicly only with your permission, and if you ask us not to contact you again, we won’t.

Who else sees it

  • Form messages are delivered by Formspree, which acts as our processor.
  • Google Analytics, provided by Google Ireland Limited, acts as our processor for website statistics, only if you accept analytics cookies. Google may process this data in the United States under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare, which process standard technical data (such as IP addresses) to deliver the pages securely.

We never sell or share your details for marketing.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that don’t lead to work are deleted after 12 months. Client records are kept for 6 years, as Irish tax law requires.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details, or object to how they’re used, by emailing hello@iterramaps.com. You can also complain to the Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie).
