Iterra Maps › How it works

From your route to finished video in four steps.

You send a route. We build the video on the real roads in your brand, you ask for changes, and we deliver it in every shape. It starts with a free sample.

The four steps

  1. You send the route

    A GPX or Ride with GPS file, a link to the tour page, or just a list of stops, with your logo and deadline.

  2. We build it

    A first version within 5 working days, on the real roads and trails, in your colours.

  3. You review

    Ask for changes to timing, labels, colours or the camera. One round is included.

  4. We deliver

    MP4s in widescreen, vertical and square, up to 4K, ready to post anywhere.

What we need from you

  • The route: a GPX or Ride with GPS file, a tour page link, or a list of stops in order
  • Your logo, ideally a PNG with a transparent background or an SVG
  • Your brand colours, or we can take them from your website
  • The date you need the videos by
  • Anything you’d like on each stop’s card: nights, distances, climbing, highlights
  • Optional: one photo per stop, that you own or have the rights to use

What you get

The files

  • MP4 video in widescreen 16:9, vertical 9:16 and square 1:1, up to 4K
  • A still poster image for brochures and email
  • An animated GIF for emails, on request
  • Silent by default, so it autoplays anywhere

Your house style

  • Your logo, colours, font and icons, set once
  • Saved, so every later video matches
  • Or start from one of our ready-made looks

Questions

What do you need from us?

Just the route: a GPX or Ride with GPS file, a link, a PDF or a list of stops. For walking and cycling, a route file gives the most exact line; for waymarked trails we can usually find the official route ourselves. Send your logo (ideally a transparent PNG or an SVG), your brand colours and your deadline. If you’d like a photo on each stop’s card, send one per stop that you own or have the rights to use.

How long does it take?

A free sample usually takes 2 to 3 working days. For a full video, you get a first version within 5 working days of sending the route, for up to five videos. For bigger orders we agree a schedule with you. Need it sooner? Ask, and we’ll tell you honestly whether we can.

How do we put the video on our website?

You get MP4 files in widescreen, vertical and square. They upload to your website, YouTube, Instagram or an email tool like any other video. If someone else looks after your website, we’re happy to answer their questions.

Can we keep our routes private?

Yes. You choose how much the line shows: the exact roads, a simplified line, or simple curves from town to town. The distances and timing stay true whichever you pick.

What if the route changes?

Send the new file. Your route and style are saved, so we remake the videos it affects at half their price.

Is there music or a voice-over?

Videos are delivered silent, so they autoplay anywhere, including on your website. You can add your own track if you want one.
