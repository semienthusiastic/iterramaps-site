Showcase · Charity ride

A two-day charity ride through the Texas Hill Country

129 miles over two days, from Dripping Springs to Comfort, with every rest stop and lunch stop on the map and the ride's sponsors on screen from start to finish. Watch it first, then see what went into it and how it would work for your own event.

01

01 · Look closer

What's packed into this video

  • Both days on the real roads

    Day 1 follows Fitzhugh Road and Pedernales Falls Road to Johnson City, then the ranch roads to Fredericksburg. Day 2 runs south on Old San Antonio Road and RM 1376 to Comfort.

  • Every rest stop and lunch stop

    Each stop gets a card with its mile marker and what's there, so riders know where the water, the food and the mechanics are.

  • The overnight, on its own card

    Fredericksburg closes Day 1 with the day's miles, and dinner and camping at the fairgrounds.

  • Each day in its own color

    Day 1 in green, Day 2 in orange, with a small key in the corner so nobody mixes them up.

  • Sponsors on screen all ride long

    One sponsor at a time in the corner while the route plays, each for a few seconds, then all of them together on the end card.

  • The climbing, mile by mile

    An elevation profile fills in along the bottom as the riders go, with the total climbed so far.

03 · Your sponsors

On screen, not just on a list

Every sponsor gets a few seconds of their own in the corner while the route plays, and a place on the end card with the others. Send us their logos and we'll place them, each on a white tile so any logo reads on any map.

Where they appear

  • In the corner during the ride, one at a time
  • On the end card, all together
  • In every shape: widescreen, vertical, square and GIF

04 · Your look

It doesn't have to. Every video wears your brand.

The same route can take any look: your colors, fonts and logo, or one of our ready-made styles to start from.

  • Cycling TrailA clean, light map with a bright route and the climbing along the bottom. The look in this video.
  • Relief ModelShaded hills, names lying on the ground and a few clouds, for big landscapes.
  • Midnight NeonA near-black map with neon roads and a glowing route, for night rides and social media.

05 · What it took

A route and a list of stops. That's all we started with.

The two days' routes, the rest stops and lunch stops, and the sponsors' logos. We checked each day against the real roads and the mile markers.

What you'd send us

  • Your route: GPX files, Ride with GPS links, or the cue sheet
  • Your rest stops and lunch stops, with their mile markers
  • Your logo, and your sponsors' logos
