The Lakeshore 330: a route video and six daily clips

A six-day ride up the Lake Michigan shore from Grand Haven to Mackinaw City. One video shows the whole route for the reveal. Six short vertical clips show one day each for the countdown.

01 · Look closer

What an event gets

  • A route video for the reveal

    The whole ride in about a minute, town by town, for announcement day and the event page.

  • A clip for each day

    Six vertical clips of 12 seconds each, one per day, for the social countdown.

  • The real roads

    Each day's towns and roads are clear before anyone clips in.

  • Made to be shared

    Riders share them, and sponsors get seen.
