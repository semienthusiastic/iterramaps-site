The Camino from Sarria to Santiago, stage by stage

The last five stages of the Camino Francés on foot: about 115 km (71 miles) from Sarria to Santiago de Compostela. Watch it first, then see what a walking video can show.

01 · Look closer

What a walking video can show

  • The trail itself

    For waymarked trails we follow the walking route, not the nearest road. A GPX file gives the most exact line.

  • A card for each stage

    Each night's town can carry a card with the distance walked and what's there.

  • The climbs

    An elevation profile can run along the bottom, so walkers see the hills before they book.

  • Your level of detail

    Show the exact trail, a simplified line or simple curves from town to town.

One itinerary is enough to start.
