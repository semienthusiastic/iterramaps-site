Alaska's Inside Passage, by small ship

A week from Vancouver to Sitka, drawn on the real sailing channels, with a card at every port. Watch it first, then see what went into it and what we'd change for your own cruises.

01

01 · Look closer

What's packed into this video

  • Sailing the real channels

    The line follows the water, through Johnstone Strait, Lynn Canal and Icy Strait, never across land.

  • A card at every port

    Each port gets a card with what to see there, as the ship arrives.

  • A camera that fits each leg

    It pulls out for the long crossing to Ketchikan and moves in for the short hop up Lynn Canal.

  • Distance counter

    The distance sailed counts up as the ship goes.

  • Title and end cards

    The cruise's name to open, and a closing card with the call to action.

  • The cruise line's brand

    Colours, fonts and a ship icon for a made-up line, Northwater Small Ship Cruises.

03 · Your look

It doesn't have to. Every video wears your brand.

The same route can take any look: your colours, fonts and logo, or one of our ready-made styles to start from.

  • Coastal ModernWhite land and deep blue water: clean and bright.

04 · Your level of detail

Then the map doesn't have to give it away

You choose how much the line shows. The distances and timing stay true whichever you pick.

  • ExactThe real route, turn by turn. Best where there is only one way to go.
  • SimplifiedThe direction of each day and the places it passes, without the turns.
  • Town to townA gentle curve between stops, like an airline route map.

05 · What it took

One itinerary. That's all we started with.

The itinerary of a 7-night small-ship cruise: the ports, the order and a line about each one. We found the sailing route through the channels ourselves.

What you'd send us

  • A link to the cruise on your website, or the itinerary
  • Your logo, and your brand colours if you have them
  • Photos for the port cards, if you'd like them
