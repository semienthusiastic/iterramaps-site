Showcase · Coach tour

A week in the Canadian Rockies by coach

Seven nights from Calgary through Banff, Lake Louise, the Icefields Parkway and Jasper, on the real highways, with a card for each day. Watch it first, then see what went into it.

01 · Look closer

What's packed into this video

  • The real highways

    The coach follows the actual roads between each overnight town, not a straight line across the map.

  • A card for each day

    Each day gets a card with the hotel, meals and highlights from the itinerary, in the tour company's own words.

  • Shaded mountains

    The Rockies are drawn in shaded relief, so the scale of the landscape lands at a glance.

  • A distance counter

    The counter adds up the distance as the coach goes, so the size of each day is clear.

  • The tour company's brand

    Colours, type and a coach icon for a made-up company, Tamarack & Pine Coachways.

  • Every shape

    Widescreen for the tour page, vertical for Reels and square for Facebook and email.
