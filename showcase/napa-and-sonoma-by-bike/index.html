Napa and Sonoma by bike, in five days

Five days of riding from Healdsburg to Sonoma on the real roads, with a card for each overnight town and the Oakville Grade climbed in 3D. Watch it first, then see what went into it and what we'd change for your own tours.

01

01 · Look closer

What's packed into this video

  • Every day on the real roads

    Each day follows the roads riders will use: Dry Creek Road, Chalk Hill Road, the Silverado Trail and the back roads through Carneros.

  • A card for each overnight town

    As the riders arrive, a card shows the nights there, the day's distance and what's in town.

  • The optional climb in its own colour

    Day 4 has two ways to Sonoma. The Oakville Grade option is drawn in gold, with a key so nobody mixes them up.

  • The big climb in 3D

    A second part follows the bike up the Oakville Grade, with the hills in 3D and an elevation profile underneath.

  • Distance in miles

    The counter adds up the miles as the bike goes, so the size of each day is clear.

  • The tour company's brand

    Wine-country colours, serif type and a bike icon for a made-up company, Vine & Valley Cycling Co.

It doesn't have to. Every video wears your brand.

The same route can take any look: your colours, fonts and logo, or one of our ready-made styles to start from.

  • Wine CountryCream land, vine greens, serif names and a burgundy line. The look in this video.

04 · Your level of detail

Then the map doesn't have to give them away

You choose how much the line shows. The distances and climbing stay true whichever you pick.

  • ExactThe real roads, turn by turn, so riders see exactly where they will go.
  • SimplifiedThe direction of each day and the places it passes, without the turns.
  • Town to townA gentle curve between overnight towns, so your routing stays yours.

Next stopSo what did it take to make this one?

05 · What it took

One itinerary. That's all we started with.

A five-day itinerary: the overnight towns, each day's ride and the optional climb. We worked out each day's route on bike-friendly roads ourselves and checked the distances.

What you'd send us

  • A link to the tour on your website, or the day-by-day itinerary
  • GPX files of your routes, if you have them
  • Your logo, and your brand colours if you have them
