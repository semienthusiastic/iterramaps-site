Questions about pricing

What does one video include?

The whole route in a video of up to 90 seconds with up to 12 stops, in widescreen, vertical and square, up to 4K, in your logo and colors, with one round of changes.

What is a short clip?

A video of one day or one leg, up to 20 seconds. Events use them for a daily countdown; tour companies use them for single days and signature climbs.

Is the sample really free?

Yes. Send one tour link and we make a 15-second sample of part of the route in your colors. There is no cost and no obligation to order.

What if the route changes?

Send the new file. Your route and style are saved, so we remake the videos it affects at half their price.

What is the small Iterra Maps mark?

Each video carries a small, semi-transparent Iterra Maps mark in one corner. If you’d rather have it without, a clean version is <span data-usd="$29" data-eur="€29">$29</span> per video.

Who owns the videos?

You can use them in your own marketing, on any channel, for as long as you like. The map data credit (© OpenStreetMap contributors) stays in the corner, as the map license requires.

How long does it take?

A free sample usually takes 2 to 3 business days. For a full video, you get a first version within 5 business days of sending the route, for up to five videos. For bigger orders we agree a schedule with you. Need it sooner? Ask, and we’ll tell you honestly whether we can.
